# Domains

## Your domains

<!-- See it in the demo: link goes here (#615) -->

Go to **My Domains**. The page has three tabs: **All**, **Active** and **Expired**.

Domain statuses:

| Status | What it means |
|--------|--------------|
| Active | Registered and working |
| Pending | Registration is being processed |
| Pending Transfer | A transfer from another registrar is in progress |
| Pending DNS | DNS is being set up |
| Expired | The domain has expired and is not active |
| Grace Period | Recently expired. Can still be renewed at the normal price. |
| Redemption | In the redemption period. Can be recovered at a higher cost. |
| Transferred Out | The domain has been transferred to another provider |
| Cancelled | The domain registration was cancelled |

When a domain is being registered the page shows **Domain Registration in Progress**. When a transfer from another registrar is in progress it shows **Domain Transfer in Progress**.

## Nameservers

Open a domain and go to its nameserver settings. Choose:

- **Use default nameservers**: uses your hosting provider's nameservers.
- **Use custom nameservers**: enter your own nameserver addresses.

Save the change. DNS changes can take up to 24 hours to take effect worldwide.

## Lock, transfer code and privacy

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| Registrar lock | Prevents accidental or unauthorised transfers; turn it off before initiating a transfer away |
| Email EPP Code | Sends the authorisation code needed to transfer the domain to another registrar |
| ID protection | Hides your personal contact details from public WHOIS lookups |

## Renew a domain

Click **Renew Now** to renew a domain before it expires. You can also turn on **auto renew**. When enabled, a renewal invoice is raised automatically before the domain expires and the domain renews once it is paid.

## Contact details

Open a domain to update the registrant contact details. Changes may be subject to a verification email from the registry.

## Transfer a domain in

To transfer a domain from another registrar to this provider, use the domain search in the [ordering](./ordering) flow, click **Transfer this domain instead** and enter the **Authorization Code** from your current registrar.
