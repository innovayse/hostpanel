# Ordering

## Choose a plan

<Steps>
<li>

Browse the hosting plans and click **Configure** on the one you want.

</li>
<li>

Choose a **Billing Cycle** — Monthly or Annual. The page shows **Setup Fees** and **Total Due Today**.

</li>
<li>

Click **Continue** to add it to your cart.

</li>
</Steps>

## Find a domain

<Steps>
<li>

Go to the domain search page and type the domain name you want.

</li>
<li>

Click **Search**. Results show **Available!** or **Already registered** for each option.

</li>
<li>

Click **Register Now** to add an available domain to your cart. If the domain is already registered elsewhere and you want to move it here, click **Transfer this domain instead** and enter the **Authorization Code** from your current registrar.

</li>
</Steps>

The pricing table shows **New Price**, **Renewal** and **Transfer** prices for each domain extension.

## Your cart

Go to **Your Cart** to review your order. You can:

- Change the **Currency** — note that changing currency clears your cart.
- Click **Remove** to remove a single item.
- Click **Clear Cart** to remove everything.
- Click **Proceed to Checkout** when you are ready to pay.

## Checkout

<!-- See it in the demo: link goes here (#615) -->

<Steps>
<li>

If you are signed in your name is shown as **Logged in as**. If you are a guest, fill in **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email Address**, **Create Password** and **Phone Number**.

</li>
<li>

Choose a **Payment Method**: one of the payment methods your provider offers, such as **Pay by bank transfer**.

</li>
<li>

Click **Place Order**.

</li>
<li>

If the total changed after you reviewed the cart, a confirmation prompt appears: "The total changed from {shown} to {charged}. Pay the new total?" Confirm to continue.

</li>
</Steps>

## After the order

The page shows **Payment Successful!**. Buttons let you **Set Up My Service** or **Manage Your Domain** depending on what you ordered.
