# Support tickets

## Open a ticket

<!-- See it in the demo: link goes here (#615) -->

<Steps>
<li>

Go to **Support** and click **Open New Ticket**.

</li>
<li>

Fill in the form:
- **Department** — choose the team you want to reach
- **Subject** — a short description of your question
- **Priority** — Low, Medium or High
- **Message** — describe the issue in detail

</li>
<li>

Click **Submit Ticket**. The page shows **Ticket Submitted!** and you receive a confirmation email.

</li>
</Steps>

## Follow up on a ticket

Go to **Support Tickets**. The page has three tabs: **All**, **Open** and **Closed**.

Ticket statuses:

| Status | What it means |
|--------|--------------|
| Open | Waiting for a response from support |
| Awaiting reply | Support has replied and is waiting for your response |
| Answered | Support has provided an answer |
| Closed | The ticket is closed |

To add a reply, open the ticket, type in the **Add Reply** box and click **Send Reply**.

A closed ticket cannot receive new replies. Open a new ticket if you need further help.

| Error | What it means |
|-------|--------------|
| `TICKET_REPLY_NOT_ALLOWED` | A closed ticket cannot receive new replies. Open a new ticket instead. |

## Announcements and knowledge base

The **Support** section also shows announcements posted by your hosting provider and a knowledge base with help articles. Check there first — your question may already be answered.
